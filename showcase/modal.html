Modal

Dialog overlay for focused tasks. A fixed backdrop dims the page while the modal panel captures attention. Includes header, scrollable body, and footer zones.

Source file: styles/components/modal.css

Modal Anatomy — .modal-overlay / .modal-panel

The modal consists of a full-viewport overlay and a centered panel. The panel is divided into three regions: .modal-header, .modal-body, and .modal-footer.

In production the .modal-overlay uses position:fixed with full viewport coverage. The demo below uses position:absolute on a constrained container for inline display.

Scrollable Body

The .modal-body has overflow-y: auto and a max-height of 60vh, so long content scrolls without the header or footer moving. The panel itself uses max-height: 90vh as a safety net.

Accessibility

  • Add role="dialog" and aria-modal="true" to .modal-overlay.
  • Connect a visible heading via aria-labelledby.
  • Move focus to the first focusable element inside the panel on open.
  • Trap focus within the panel while it is open.
  • Close on Escape and on backdrop click.
  • Restore focus to the trigger element on close.
